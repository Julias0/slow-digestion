# What is this?

Slow digestion is meant to be an application that helps you make sense of your monthly spend. 

A lot of people like to write their transations in a notebook. I have found the process very hard and annoying.

An easier option always was to spend a day going through the bank statement and analyze where the spend is going.

The problem?

Bank statements dont make a lot of sense with the 100,000 UPI transactions that take place now.

This application is to help there.

It will cross your bank statement with your grocery bills, help you tag your spends etc.

That way, you know where your money is going
